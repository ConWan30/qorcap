QORCAP

HDMI in. Hash chain out.

A lean Windows recorder for your capture card. Every ~2 s segment is SHA-256 chained and checkpointed in QorTime's format. It reads the Madden 27 scorebug, confirms the final from the end-of-game menu, and can submit the match to the official QorTime referee, which re-checks it on its own before it signs anything. If the card freezes or gets replugged, it reopens itself.

01 · Signal to seal

From HDMI to a receipt in six moves.

Scroll to run the pipeline. Each stage is something QorCap actually writes to disk or serves on its local API.

Signal → Seal

STAGE 1 / 6

The card

Your console's HDMI hits a USB capture card. QorCap opens it over DirectShow and drives ffmpeg as a child process: constant frame rate, hardware H.264 (h264_mf), AAC.

STAGE 2 / 6

Segments

The stream is cut into fragmented MP4 segments of about 2 seconds, flushed as they're written. A power cut loses at most the last fragment.

STAGE 3 / 6

The chain

Each segment's exact bytes get a SHA-256, linked to the previous head and appended to manifest.jsonl. Change one byte anywhere and every later link stops matching.

STAGE 4 / 6

Checkpoints

On segments, score changes and finals, QorCap emits checkpoints in QorTime's format, served at /checkpoints and streamed over SSE, ready for a referee to co-sign.

STAGE 5 / 6

Receipt

At the whistle it writes a match bundle (bundle.json, clips, receipt.draft.json) and Start Match signs receipt.json on this PC. Signed locally, it verifies as SELF_ATTESTED.

STAGE 6 / 6

Verify

qorcap verify-bundle rehashes everything and catches any changed byte. For an independent verdict, qorcap submit sends the bundle to the official QorTime referee.

03 · Live official challenges

  
A nonce, pressed on the pad.

  
Start the match with --official-challenges and the QorTime referee issues three signed, timed challenges: kickoff, halftime and Q4. You answer each one with a button pattern on the controller. The pattern can't be known before the referee issues its nonce, and each challenge carries a chain-head anchor showing the earlier video already existed.

  
Between challenges, QorCap has the referee timestamp the chain head every 60 s (--no-anchors turns it off). Without official challenges the best official result is PARTIAL: the referee can show the bytes are what you recorded, not that they were recorded live.

  
QorCap's 2026-10-16 sync report lists official challenges on a real DualSense as not yet exercised in a real match. Local challenges still work too: a nonce sent to POST /challenge is checkpointed, and with --stamp it's burned into the video itself.

05 · Final read, confirmed

  
The final is read, not guessed.

  
The scorebug can vanish before the last play's points are drawn, so QorCap reads the final from Madden's end-of-game menu, with the same template-digit method as QorTime's reader and every digit cross-checked. Only a stable read sets result.method = "end_menu_read" and final_confirmed: true.

  
A scorebug-only final stays unconfirmed, and Start Match keeps recording up to 120 s for the menu. On online matches the name tags cover the away score, so the menu can only corroborate the scorebug's final, never read a new one. A one-play jump past the last scorebug read needs corroborating evidence (a score-change clip, haptics or inputs near the score). If the live read misses it, Start Match checks the recording's last minutes at stop, and Finalize Last Match can redo it offline.

 






 

  
06 · Submit for Verification

  
Metadata, then video on request.

  
A bundle signed only by your laptop is SELF_ATTESTED. To aim for VERIFIED, double-click Submit for Verification, answer y at the end of Start Match, or run qorcap submit. No account is needed: the request is signed with this PC's install key, the same device key that recorded the match.

  
    
   
  1. 1
    QorCap fetches a single-use nonce, solves a small proof-of-work and signs the request and the bundle's manifest hash with the install key.
    2. 
   
  2. 2
    It uploads the small metadata only: manifest, checkpoints, inputs, receipts. No video yet.
    3. 
   
  3. 3
    The referee re-checks every chain and signature itself, commits to them, then picks segments: the init segment, the last 240 s, segments around each challenge and 16 random ones.
    4. 
   
  4. 4
    QorCap uploads those. The referee hashes the bytes against the chain, re-reads the final with QorCap's own readers and re-runs the challenges. Only if every check passes does it countersign.
    5.

07 · The official referee

 
One root, kept offline.

 
The QorTime referee's root key qortime-v1 stays offline. It signs a short-lived delegation for an online key, and that online key does the countersigning. Every countersigned receipt goes into a public, hash-chained transparency log. QorCap checks the delegation and the revocation list locally before it uploads anything.

 

  
Offline root
qortime-v1
Signs delegation certificates and the revocation list. Never online.

  

  
Delegation cert
≤ 90 days
Scope official-review. Caps the verdict at VERIFIED or PARTIAL. Revocable with one root-signed entry.

  

  
Online key
Countersigns
Signs only after its own review passes. Refuses to sign outside the cert's window.

  

  
Transparency log
Append-only
Each receipt is logged before release. The log head is anchored in the QorTime repo.

 

 

  
VERIFIED
Official key, every check passes, and the kickoff, halftime and Q4 official challenges re-verify, with enough motion.

  
PARTIAL
Official key, but a check is only partial, a phase has no passed official challenge, or motion is too low.

  
SELF_ATTESTED
The referee key isn't on QorTime's allowlist, or is revoked. Every local Start Match receipt lands here.

  
FAIL
Any failed check, a missing or forged review, a bad chain signature, or any normal verify failure.

 

 
Rules from QorTime's REFEREE_SERVICE.md, REFEREE_KEYS.md and TRUSTED_REFEREES.md. The Python and browser verifiers apply the same rules.






 
08 · Supporting evidence

 
Extra signals, labelled.

 
Alongside the video chain, QorCap records evidence that helps a reviewer, and says plainly what each piece is worth.

 

  
Audio precheck
Game audio levels
Start Match records 4 s of the card's audio before the match (audio_precheck.json), and Start Match and Finalize measure the recorded audio (audio_check.json). A silent card gets a loud warning with the likely fixes.

  
Controller input
Inputs on the video clock
With the DualSense on the PS5 over Bluetooth and USB-plugged into the laptop, QorCap logs its HID reports to inputs.jsonl. Each chunk's hash goes into that segment's checkpoint, so the inputs are part of the chain. Advisory evidence, not proof

  
Haptics
Rumble from the IMU
The PS5 sends rumble over Bluetooth, which the laptop can't see, so QorCap watches the pad's gyro and accelerometer for vibration bursts (haptics.jsonl). Advisory evidence, not proof

 

 
The input proof's human-likeness score is a hand-set heuristic, and input-video alignment is reported against a chance baseline. Neither shows who held the controller.

10 · Why not OBS

  
Freezes are survivable now.

  
The usual failure: a cheap capture card's driver hangs, and the recorder sits on a frozen device forever. QorCap runs ffmpeg as a child process, so a hung driver can only freeze ffmpeg, never QorCap. A watchdog kills the child after 4 s without data and reopens the card. The recording continues in video.part2.mp4, and the chain carries on across parts.

  

   
Typical recorder
  • Hangs with the driver
  • Replug often needs restart
  • No integrity record
  • Big general-purpose install

   
QorCap
  • Watchdog kills & reopens
  • Polls for replugged card
  • Chain continues across parts
  • One small exe + ffmpeg

11 · How it fits QorTime

 
QorCap records. QorTime signs.

 
QorCap is the capture half. It produces evidence; QorTime's referee re-checks it and signs, and anyone can check the result on the QorTime verifier. More on the QorTime site and in the QorTime repo.

 

 
 
 
QorCap evidencesigned / verified (QorTime)

 

 

  Compatibility
  
QorCap and QorTime share one contracts registry. The canonical copy lives in QorTime's schema/contracts/ (compat.json plus JSON Schemas), and QorCap vendors a byte-identical copy in contracts/ with SHA256SUMS and CONTRACTS_PIN. Tests on both sides fail on drift. Each release zip bundles a pinned QorTime copy, and the official referee accepts submissions only from QorCap 0.3.10 or newer. Details in the sync report.

 






 

  
12 · Quickstart

  
Double-click Start Match.

  
Unzip, plug the PS5 into the USB3.0 Video card, double-click Start Match. On its initial run it downloads ffmpeg and sets up Python by itself. One window shows the score, clock, segments and clips; it stops when the final is read from the end-of-game menu (or press Enter), signs the receipt, verifies it, offers to submit it and opens the match folder. No card handy? Double-click Test Without Card.

  
Trust level: a receipt signed only on your PC is SELF_ATTESTED. It proves the video wasn't changed after recording, not who held the controller. For an official verdict, submit it to the QorTime referee.

13 · Honest limits

 
What it doesn't prove.

 
QorCap is evidence about footage, not about people. Here is exactly where the line is.

 

  
Proves
Integrity & timing
Which exact video bytes, in what order, seen when. With official challenges and anchors, the official referee ties the chain to real time.

  
Does not prove
Who is playing
No proof of player identity. Inputs and haptics show a controller plugged into this laptop was in use, not who held it.

  
No attestation
Software keys
Install keys are software Ed25519 keys with no hardware attestation. They bind a submission to the recorder and deter floods; they don't prove a genuine device.

  
Sampled
Spot-check
By default the referee inspects the tail, the challenge windows and 16 random segments. A laptop missing a fraction f of segments passes the random part with probability (1−f)16. Unsampled content isn't inspected. --full uploads everything.

  
Before hashing
Pre-capture edits
Footage fabricated or edited before QorCap hashed it, outside anchored windows, isn't detected. The local countersignatures on your PC don't prove liveness.

  
Platform
Windows only
Windows x64, DirectShow, tuned on one generic "USB3.0 Video" card. Madden 27 only; Sunday Night and Thursday Night scorebugs are untested.

  
Advisory
Heuristics
The human-likeness score and haptic thresholds are hand-set, not trained. The referee re-checks input hashes and counts, but doesn't re-run that model.

  
Scope
No chain, no token
Nothing goes on a blockchain. The "chain" is a SHA-256 hash chain in local files; the referee's transparency log is a hash-chained file it publishes.

  
This page
Simulations, labelled
Every live-looking demo here runs in your browser. No stats, no testimonials, no real match data.
