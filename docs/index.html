QORCAP

HDMI in. Hash chain out.

A lean Windows recorder for your capture card. Every ~2 s segment is SHA-256 chained, checkpointed in QorTime's format, and sealed into a match bundle with a draft receipt ready for QorTime signing. If the card freezes or gets replugged, it reopens itself.

01 · Signal to seal

From HDMI to a receipt in six moves.

Scroll to run the pipeline. Each stage is something QorCap actually writes to disk or serves on its local API.

Signal → Seal

STAGE 1 / 6

The card

Your console's HDMI hits a USB capture card. QorCap opens it over DirectShow and drives ffmpeg as a child process: constant frame rate, hardware H.264 (h264_mf), AAC.

STAGE 2 / 6

Segments

The stream is cut into fragmented MP4 segments of about 2 seconds, flushed as they're written. A power cut loses at most the last fragment.

STAGE 3 / 6

The chain

Each segment's exact bytes get a SHA-256, linked to the previous head and appended to manifest.jsonl. Change one byte anywhere and every later link stops matching.

STAGE 4 / 6

Checkpoints

On segments, score changes and finals, QorCap emits checkpoints in QorTime's format, served at /checkpoints and streamed over SSE, ready for a referee to co-sign.

STAGE 5 / 6

Draft receipt

At the whistle it writes a match bundle: bundle.json, clips and a receipt.draft.json. It stays unsigned until QorTime's referee step signs it.

STAGE 6 / 6

Verify

qorcap verify-bundle rehashes everything and catches any changed byte. The signed receipt is checked on QorTime's browser verifier.

03 · Not pre-recorded

  
A nonce, burned into the footage.

  
A QorTime referee sends a random nonce to POST /challenge. QorCap records when it arrived, which segment was being written and the chain head at that instant, and adds a checkpoint containing the nonce.

  
With --stamp, the nonce and its button pattern are drawn into the recorded video itself. Footage after that point could not have existed before the nonce did.

 






 

  
04 · Auto-clips

  
The scorebug calls the clips.

  
QorTime's own scorebug reader runs inline on a 4 fps snapshot. Each confirmed score change saves a clip from the in-RAM replay ring. At the end it saves a final clip and records why it thinks the game ended: final_basis = clock_zero or scorebug_gone.

06 · Why not OBS

  
Freezes are survivable now.

  
The usual failure: a cheap capture card's driver hangs, and the recorder sits on a frozen device forever. QorCap runs ffmpeg as a child process, so a hung driver can only freeze ffmpeg, never QorCap. A watchdog kills the child after 4 s without data and reopens the card. The recording continues in video.part2.mp4, and the chain carries on across parts.

  

   
Typical recorder
  • Hangs with the driver
  • Replug often needs restart
  • No integrity record
  • Big general-purpose install

   
QorCap
  • Watchdog kills & reopens
  • Polls for replugged card
  • Chain continues across parts
  • One 1.6 MB exe + ffmpeg

07 · How it fits QorTime

 
QorCap records. QorTime signs.

 
QorCap is the capture half. It produces evidence; QorTime's agent and referee add keys and signatures, and anyone can check the result on the QorTime verifier.

08 · Quickstart

  
Five minutes to a bundle.

  
Unzip, fetch ffmpeg, find your card, record. The scorebug sidecar needs Python 3.11+ and a QorTime checkout. If h264_mf fails, use --encoder libx264; if the card refuses raw 720p, try --input-format mjpeg.

  
Download zipFull README

 

 


# PowerShell · unzip to C:\QorCap first
cd C:\QorCap
Unblock-File .\qorcap.exe, .\get-ffmpeg.ps1
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\get-ffmpeg.ps1
.\qorcap.exe devices        # expect "USB3.0 Video"

# record a match bundle, nonce stamping on
.\qorcap.exe record --device "USB3.0 Video" `
  --res 1280x720 --fps 30 --bundle C:\QorCap\match1 `
  --match-id <id> --role home --player-id psn:<you> --stamp

# second window: scorebug sidecar
py .\sidecar\qorcap_reader.py --qortime C:\src\qortime

.\qorcap.exe stop
.\qorcap.exe verify-bundle C:\QorCap\match1






 
09 · Honest limits

 
What it doesn't prove.

 
QorCap is evidence about footage, not about people. Here is exactly where the line is.

 

  
Proves
Integrity & timing
Which exact video bytes, in what order, seen when, and (once a referee co-signs) no later than the referee's clock.

  
Does not prove
Who is playing
No proof of player identity. Player keys, the HID nonce response and dual-attested mode are QorTime's job.

  
Status
v0.1.0, untested on hardware
Cross-compiled and tested on Linux with a test-pattern source. Not yet run on Windows with a real card.

  
Receipt
Unsigned draft
receipt.draft.json carries no signatures until QorTime's referee step fills in keys and signs it.

  
Scope
No chain, no token
Nothing goes on a blockchain. The "chain" is a local SHA-256 hash chain in a text file.

  
This page
Simulations, labelled
Every live-looking demo here runs in your browser. No stats, no testimonials, no real match data.
